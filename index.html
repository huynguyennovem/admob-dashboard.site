How it works

Three steps from connect to daily tracking.

Connect Google

Sign in with the Google account that has AdMob access. The app requests readonly reporting scope only.

Sync AdMob

Pull live mediated revenue, impressions, match rate, eCPM, apps, and ad units from the AdMob API.

Track daily

Switch ranges, refresh on pull, and drill into apps whenever you want a closer look.
