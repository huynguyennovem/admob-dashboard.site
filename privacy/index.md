# Privacy Policy — Admob Dashboard

**Last updated:** September 6, 2026

Admob Dashboard (“the app”) is a personal mobile dashboard for viewing AdMob revenue metrics. This policy explains what information is involved when you use the app and this website.

## 1. Overview

Admob Dashboard connects to your Google account so it can read AdMob reporting data. The app is designed as a personal client: metrics are requested from Google’s AdMob API on your behalf and shown in the app UI.

## 2. Google Sign-In

To use the core features, you sign in with Google. Authentication is handled by Google Sign-In. We request the AdMob readonly scope:

- `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admob.readonly`

This scope allows the app to read AdMob account and reporting information. It does not allow the app to change your AdMob settings or publish ads.

You can disconnect your account from within the app. You can also revoke access at any time in your Google Account permissions.

## 3. AdMob reporting data

After you connect, the app requests live reporting metrics from the Google AdMob Reporting API. Depending on your AdMob account, this may include estimated earnings, requests, impressions, match rate, eCPM, show rate, apps, ad units, countries, platforms, and related performance breakdowns for the date ranges you select.

This data originates from Google. The app displays it for your personal use. The app does not upload your AdMob metrics to a separate Admob Dashboard backend for storage or resale.

## 4. Data we do not collect

- We do not ask you to upload files through the app.
- We do not operate a multi-tenant cloud account system for this product.
- As of the date above, the app does not integrate third-party analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs beyond Google Sign-In and the AdMob Reporting API used for your own revenue metrics.

If analytics, crash reporting, or ads are added later, this policy will be updated before those features ship in store builds.

## 5. This website

The marketing site at admob-dashboard.site provides product information, support pages, and legal documents. It is a static site. Standard web server or host logs (such as IP address and user agent) may be processed by the hosting provider as part of delivering the site.

## 6. Children’s privacy

Admob Dashboard is intended for adults who manage AdMob publishing accounts. It is not directed to children.

## 7. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change when we do. Continued use of the app or site after an update means you accept the revised policy.
