## FAQ

### How do I connect my Google account?

Open the app and tap Connect account. Sign in with the Google account that has access to your AdMob publisher account. The app requests AdMob readonly access only.

### What Google Cloud / AdMob setup do I need?

You only need a Google account that can sign in to AdMob. Open AdMob with that account in a browser to confirm access — no separate AdMob API enablement is required for normal use.

### Why don’t I see revenue after signing in?

Confirm the signed-in Google account can open your AdMob account in a browser and that it has reporting data for the selected date range. Then pull to refresh in the app.

### What data does the app read?

Reporting metrics such as estimated earnings, impressions, match rate, eCPM, apps, and ad units — via the AdMob Reporting API. See the Privacy Policy: https://admob-dashboard.site/privacy/

### Can I disconnect?

Yes. Use the disconnect / sign-out control in the app, and optionally revoke AdGauge - AdMob Dashboard in your Google Account permissions.
