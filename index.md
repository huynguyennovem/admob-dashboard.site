# Admob Dashboard

> Admob Dashboard is a personal mobile app that connects your Google account and shows live AdMob estimated earnings, ads performance, apps, and ad units — without opening the AdMob console.

This is the marketing site for Admob Dashboard at [https://admob-dashboard.site/](https://admob-dashboard.site/).

**Status:** Coming soon on Google Play and the App Store.

## Product summary

- Platforms: Android and iOS
- Auth: Google Sign-In with AdMob readonly scope (`admob.readonly`)
- Data: Live metrics from the Google AdMob Reporting API
- Audience: Indie and small-studio AdMob publishers

## Key features

1. Estimated earnings for today, yesterday, this month, and last month
2. Ads activity metrics: earnings, requests, impressions, match rate, eCPM, show rate
3. Your dashboard with flexible ranges, revenue trend, and top ad units
4. Per-app detail: formats, countries, platforms, lifetime and custom ranges
5. Light and dark themes; pull-to-refresh
